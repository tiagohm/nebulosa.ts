# Meeus Algorithms

Use these chapter-style calculations from Meeus's _Astronomical Algorithms_ to reproduce textbook results or estimate astronomical geometry. Each topic states its own model, input units, and accuracy limits.

[Meeus Numerical Helpers]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/numerical-helpers.md %}) supplies chapter-style interpolation, fitting, iteration, and shared formulas.

[Meeus Calendar]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/calendar.md %}) converts chapter-style calendar labels, Julian days, and modeled TT dates.

[Meeus Easter Dates]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/easter-dates.md %}) calculates Easter Sunday in the Gregorian or Julian calendar.

[Meeus Sidereal Time]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/sidereal-time.md %}) evaluates chapter-style Greenwich sidereal time in seconds of time.

[Meeus Globe Ellipsoid]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/globe-ellipsoid.md %}) computes chapter-style Earth radii, parallax factors, and surface distances.

[Meeus Coordinate Transforms]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/coordinate-transforms.md %}) converts chapter-style ecliptic, equatorial, horizontal, and B1950 Galactic angles.

[Meeus Precession]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/precession.md %}) rotates chapter-style sky coordinates and orbital elements between Julian epochs.

[Meeus Nutation and Obliquity]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/nutation-and-obliquity.md %}) evaluates chapter-style nutation angles and mean obliquity.

[Meeus Apparent Place of a Star]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/apparent-place-of-a-star.md %}) reduces a mean stellar position with Meeus precession, nutation, and annual aberration.

[Meeus Parallactic Angle]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/parallactic-angle.md %}) evaluates chapter-style parallactic and horizon-intersection angles.

[Meeus Topocentric Parallax]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/topocentric-parallax.md %}) shifts a geocentric Meeus body position to an observer using Earth parallax factors.

[Meeus Refraction Formulas]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/refraction-formulas.md %}) provides chapter-style scalar corrections between true and apparent altitude.

[Meeus Approximate Rise, Transit, and Set]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/approximate-rise-transit-and-set.md %}) estimates one UT1 day's crossings and upper transit from apparent positions.

[Meeus Solar Day Clock]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/solar-day-clock.md %}) estimates solar noon, limb events, twilight, and golden-hour boundaries for a UT1 day.

[Meeus Solar Coordinates]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/solar-coordinates.md %}) evaluates short and VSOP87E geocentric solar positions and vectors.

[Meeus Equinoxes and Solstices]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/equinoxes-and-solstices.md %}) estimates season boundaries from Meeus tables or a VSOP87E solar-longitude refinement.

[Meeus Equation of Time]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/equation-of-time.md %}) calculates the Meeus apparent-minus-mean solar-time angle.

[Meeus Keplerian Elements]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/keplerian-elements.md %}) solves chapter-style Kepler equations and evaluates fixed solar-orbit elements.

[Meeus Geocentric Planet Positions]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/geocentric-planet-positions.md %}) explains the heliocentric planet coordinates that feed Meeus geocentric reductions.

[Meeus Apsis and Node Passages]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/apsis-and-node-passages.md %}) estimates planetary apsides and fixed-orbit node crossing times.

[Meeus Planetary Phenomena]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/planetary-phenomena.md %}) estimates chapter-table conjunctions, oppositions, Mercury elongations, and a Mars station.

[Meeus Conjunction Interpolation]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/conjunction-interpolation.md %}) locates coordinate conjunctions from five supplied position samples.

[Meeus Alignment Geometry]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/alignment-geometry.md %}) measures separations, dates sampled great-circle crossings, and encloses compact triples.

[Meeus Illuminated Fraction]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/illuminated-fraction.md %}) computes planetary phase angles, lit disk fractions, and bright-limb direction.

[Meeus Planetary Magnitudes]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/planetary-magnitudes.md %}) estimates visual brightness from distances, phase, and Saturn-ring geometry.

[Meeus Solar Disk Physical Ephemeris]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/solar-disk-physical-ephemeris.md %}) computes solar-disk orientation and approximate Carrington rotation starts.

[Meeus Mars Disk]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/mars-disk.md %}) describes the pole, central meridian, size, and illumination of Mars's apparent disk.

[Meeus Jupiter Disk]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/jupiter-disk.md %}) documents System I and II central meridians and Jupiter's apparent pole angle.

[Meeus Galilean Satellite Positions]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/galilean-satellite-positions.md %}) projects Jupiter's four large moons with the chapter approximation or E5 terms.

[Meeus Saturnian Satellite Positions]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/saturnian-satellite-positions.md %}) projects eight Saturnian moons with the chapter 46 Dourneau model.

[Meeus Saturn Ring Geometry]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/saturn-ring-geometry.md %}) gives Saturn's geocentric ring opening, pole angle, and projected outer-ring size.

[Meeus Lunar Position]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/lunar-position.md %}) evaluates chapter 47 geocentric lunar coordinates, distance, and orbital longitudes.

[Meeus Lunar Illumination]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/lunar-illumination.md %}) estimates geocentric lunar phase angle and lit disk fraction.

[Meeus Lunar Libration and Surface Lighting]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/lunar-libration-and-surface-lighting.md %}) gives Meeus geocentric libration, subsolar coordinates, and sunlight at lunar sites.

[Meeus Angular Semidiameters]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/angular-semidiameters.md %}) estimates the apparent radii of planets, the Sun, Moon, and asteroids.

[Meeus Stellar Magnitude Arithmetic]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/stellar-magnitude-arithmetic.md %}) combines stellar magnitudes, compares brightness, and applies the distance modulus.

[Meeus Binary-star Apparent Orbit]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/binary-star-apparent-orbit.md %}) projects visual-binary elements to position angle and angular separation.

[Meeus Sundial Geometry]({% link astronomy/meeus-algorithms/sundial-geometry.md %}) lays out sampled hour lines for general, equatorial, horizontal, and vertical sundials.
